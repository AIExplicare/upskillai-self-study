# UpskillAI Self Study

Learning AI doesn't have to be overwhelming. Microlearning gives you quick, actionable lessons—perfect for busy people. Small steps, big progress!

A 20-week applied AI self-study companion app (installable web app) with module readings, free lectures, foundational papers, 147 numbered references, a weekly milestone tracker, and a 10–15 minute daily micro-learning track (100 lessons with spaced review).

Modeled on the public syllabus of MIT Open Learning's Universal AI program. Not affiliated with or endorsed by MIT.

Install on iPhone: open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Progress is stored locally on your device.
